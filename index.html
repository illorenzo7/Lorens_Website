Loren Matilsky

Astrophysicist · University of California, Santa Cruz

I study astrophysical "dynamos" — how stars and planets like the Sun and Earth form their large-scale magnetic fields. This magnetism is often the central driver of star--planet interactions and has important consequences for the habitability of distant exoplanets.
